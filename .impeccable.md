# Stackle — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
The Bayne family — adults, teens, and kids of mixed skill, playing solo rounds on their own phones (touch) and laptops (keyboard), any time of day: kitchen table, couch, waiting rooms. Sessions are short (2–10 min). The job to be done is light, recurring family rivalry: "beat Dad's Chill-mode record," not ranked competition. State of mind: relaxed, playful, a little mischievous.

### Brand Personality
**Tactile, warm, mischievous.** The game should feel like a beloved physical object — a wooden block puzzle that lives on the coffee table — not software. Emotional goals: delight on drop, warmth on celebration, a grin at the family in-jokes. Never stressful, never babyish.

### Aesthetic Direction
Toy-like / tactile. Chunky pieces with physical presence (soft dimensional shading, subtle grain — blocks you want to pick up), springy-but-smooth motion, generous rounded-but-not-blobby geometry. Deeply personal to the Bayne family: named players with avatars/colors, per-person celebration lines and in-jokes, room for hand-drawn touches (kin to The Odd Card's spirit). Supports **both light and dark themes** via system preference — light reads as blond wood and daylight, dark as evening living-room lamplight; same toy warmth in both.

Anti-references (must NOT be): hyper-competitive/stressful (no panic flashing, no leaderboard shaming); childish/preschool-coded; a generic browser Tetris clone; feature-bloated (one great mode, done impeccably).

### Design Principles
1. **Blocks are the brand.** The tetrominoes themselves carry the visual identity — everything else stays quiet so the toys shine.
2. **Kind competition.** Records celebrated, never rubbed in. Losing feels like "one more go," not defeat.
3. **Physical, not skeuomorphic kitsch.** Dimensional and tactile through light, shadow, and motion — not fake wood textures pasted everywhere.
4. **Personal beats polished.** A hand-drawn family touch outranks another generic gradient. It should be unmistakably the Baynes' game.
5. **One great mode.** Ruthless scope: solo play, three difficulties, family leaderboard. Nothing else until that is impeccable.
